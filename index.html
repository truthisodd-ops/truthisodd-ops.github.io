How our videos are made

Truth Is Odd uses its own software, the Truth Is Odd Autopilot, to research topics, write scripts, check facts against reliable sources and produce short videos. Narration, scripts and visuals are created with the help of AI tools and are labelled as AI-generated or synthetic content on every platform. Every fact is checked before a video is published.

The Autopilot only publishes videos to the Truth Is Odd accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. It does not access, collect or store data from viewers or other users.
