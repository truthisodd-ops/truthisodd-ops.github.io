Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains how Truth Is Odd ("we") handles data on this website and in the Truth Is Odd Autopilot application (the "App"). Contact: truthisodd@gmail.com.

1. What the App does

The App is an internal tool used only by Truth Is Odd. It creates short educational videos and uploads them to our own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

2. Data the App accesses

  • Our own account authorization. When we connect our YouTube, TikTok or Instagram account, the platform gives the App an access token. The App uses it only to upload our videos and to read basic statistics (views, likes, comments count) of our own videos.
  • Public trend data. The App reads publicly available information about trending videos (titles, view counts) to choose topics. It does not collect personal data about the people who made those videos.

The App does not access, collect, store or share personal data of viewers or any other users.

3. Where data is stored

Access tokens and video files are stored only on our own computer. They are never sold, shared or transferred to third parties, except to the platform the video is being uploaded to.

4. Google and YouTube

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using our channel you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. We use the scope youtube.upload only to upload videos to our own channel. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. TikTok and Instagram

Uploads to TikTok and Instagram use their official developer APIs. Their own privacy policies apply: TikTok Privacy Policy, Instagram Privacy Policy.

6. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Your rights

If you are in the EU, you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we do not collect personal data of visitors or viewers, there is usually nothing to request. For any question, write to truthisodd@gmail.com.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
